SCALING
=====================================================================
The pipeline's fidelity computation has two tiers — exact (dense Operator
trace, n <= fidelity_exact_threshold, default 13) and approximate
(Monte-Carlo above it). All fixes below were redundant-computation fixes,
not new algorithms, verified byte-identical or algebraically exact before
adoption.

- 2026-08-25: pivoted to a fixed 8-qubit benchmark set after 12/16/20-qubit
  attempts repeatedly hung (deferred rather than debugged blind).
- 2026-08-26: profiled the injection stage and found its three strategies
  each rebuilt/resimulated whole circuits every trial when a cheap
  closed-form/cached shortcut existed: sa_injection's energy check compares
  a candidate to its own unchanged base, so the base cancels algebraically
  (~1000x+, exact); fidelity_driven_injection caches the candidate/target
  operator product and updates it incrementally via a partial-trace
  identity (~40x+, exact, bit-identical); stochastic_injection's one-gate-
  diff fidelity reduces to a 4x4 local trace independent of circuit size
  (~900x+, exact). Result: qaoa_maxcut n=12/13 pilots (previously
  infeasible) completed in <10 min; n=12 became practical at full GA
  settings for all 5 families (~80-240s/run).
- 2026-08-27: cached the 5 reporting-level fidelity checks' shared
  target-circuit operator (was rebuilt every call) — ~1.4x faster at
  n=12/13, byte-identical.
- 2026-08-27: replaced the approximate tier's SWAP-test estimator with an
  algebraically equivalent "fidelity-echo" circuit (same quantity
  |<psi|U^dagger V|psi>|^2, computed on n qubits instead of 2n+1, no
  CSWAP-induced entanglement) — the SWAP test's own construction, not
  target-circuit entanglement, was what made it exponentially expensive
  for entangled families. Verified against exact values and the old
  estimator; a 5+min/call case now takes ~0.06s. Renamed fidelity_backend
  swap_test_mc -> echo_test_mc, CLI --fidelity-swap-* -> --fidelity-echo-*.
- 2026-08-27: mapped the new backend's reach — qaoa_maxcut is the one
  family still genuinely hard past ~n=16 (a physical entanglement limit,
  not software); the other 4 scale to n=20-32+. Root-caused why QAOA still
  stalled at n=16: fidelity_driven_injection's max_trials was hardcoded at
  300 with no incremental-cache fast path above the approximate threshold.
  Exposed --fidelity-driven-max-trials; a stuck n=16 run completed in ~200s
  once it and reporting sample/shot counts were turned down.
- 2026-08-27: gave fidelity_driven_injection's per-trial loop its own fast
  path above the exact tier (up to statevector_fast_path_threshold,
  default 24): each trial only needs <chi|G_trial^dagger|phi> for a
  handful of fixed random product states, computed EXACTLY (no shot noise)
  via _statevector_pair_reduce/_apply_local_gate_to_statevector as an
  O(2^n) partial trace on cached state vectors (chi updated incrementally
  on acceptance). Verified 3 ways (brute-force outer-product, direct
  re-simulation, and convergence against the old MPS estimator at high
  shot count). The n=16 QAOA case above now runs the full 300 trials in
  ~21s (was ~175s at 20 trials); total wall clock dropped from "didn't
  finish in 280s" to ~50s. New flag --fidelity-driven-statevector-
  threshold; new meta field fidelity_driven_tier. Did NOT yet touch the 5
  pipeline-level reporting calls (fixed next).
- 2026-08-27: gave those 5 reporting calls the same exact-statevector
  option, as an explicit opt-in (benchmarked first: statevector is ~150x+
  faster than MPS for qaoa_maxcut at n=16, but ~250-400x SLOWER than MPS
  for w_state/hw_efficient_ansatz at n=24 — entanglement-dependent,
  opposite strengths, so no auto-detection). New
  approximate_gate_fidelity_statevector_mc + safe_fidelity_between_circuits'
  approximate_backend param ("mps" default, "statevector" new); CLI
  --fidelity-approximate-backend. Verified bit-identical against exact
  ground truth and convergent against the old estimator at high shot
  count. The n=20 QAOA case that previously timed out now completes in
  37.8s.
- 2026-08-28: raised run_sweep.py's CIRCUIT_QUBIT_SIZES grid via a real
  pilot per family (not just the cost benchmarks above, which never
  checked whether reported fidelity was informative). Every family but
  weak_random failed: w_state@20, hw_efficient_ansatz@20, qaoa_maxcut@16
  completed fine but at/below the echo estimator's resolution floor
  (1/1024 at defaults — w_state hit 0.000977, the other two hit exactly
  0.0); qft@20 never finished (killed after 47+ min) because its
  inherently global entanglement left 180 cross-block gates to reinject
  (vs 3-20 for other families) and hit the same MPS wall QAOA needed
  statevector for. Fixed per family, validated with real reruns:
  qaoa_maxcut and qft -> statevector backend (entanglement-agnostic, no
  shot noise); w_state and hw_efficient_ansatz -> --fidelity-echo-
  samples/shots raised 8->32/128->2048 (floor to 1/65536, MPS was never
  the problem there). Results: qaoa_maxcut@16 91.7s (was 236-256s),
  fidelity_final 2.2e-05 (was 0.0); qft@20 354.0s (was a 47+min kill),
  6.7e-05; w_state@20 226.3s (was ~60-70s), 9.2e-05 (was 0.000977, still
  floor); hw_efficient_ansatz@20 204.6s (was ~50-60s), 3.2e-04 (was 0.0).
  Wired into run_sweep.py as CIRCUIT_FIDELITY_SETTINGS, applied ONLY above
  fidelity_exact_threshold (an early unconditional version would have
  silently duplicated n=8/12 data — caught by a smoke test). Grid is now
  weak_random/w_state/qft/hw_efficient_ansatz=[8,12,20],
  qaoa_maxcut=[8,12,16] (150 configs for `python run_sweep.py`).

RESEARCH ANGLE CHOSEN AND IMPLEMENTED: SMS-EMOA + operator/hybrid
ablations (2026-08-25)
=====================================================================
Closed Phase 2's biggest blocker (no research-contribution angle). Picked,
over a noise/crosstalk-aware objective: SMS-EMOA as a new per-block
optimizer vs. NSGA-II, plus mutation-operator (GECCO 2025, arXiv
2504.06413) and pure-GA-vs-hybrid-GA+LAS (GECCO 2025 companion, arXiv
2504.17561) ablations — reuses already-tested code (compute_hv, the
injection stage's pluggable-strategy pattern), no new dependencies or
noise modeling.

Implemented optimise_block_smsemoa (binary tournament by Pareto rank;
environmental selection stacks fronts then trims the boundary front by
least-hypervolume-loss, hand-rolled rather than pymoo's SMSEMOA class, by
choice); mutation_scheme ("point"/"swap_add"/"swap_add_delete" via new
mut_swap/mut_addition/mut_deletion, swap schemes make chromosomes
variable-length); hybrid_las (runs the previously-dead LAS local search on
the GA's winner). Wired through the pipeline, CLI, sweep, and aggregation.
Fixed a pre-existing bug: optimise_block_nsga2 was double-appending to
history_moo every generation. SMS-EMOA's hypervolume-trim wasn't a
wall-clock blocker (8.2s vs NSGA-II's 10.7s, same-size test).

Ran 3 staged single-axis sweeps (not a 600+-run factorial), 8-qubit x
5-circuit x 5-seed, injection_method=stochastic, ~175 runs:

(a) nsga2 vs smsemoa (point, no hybrid_las):
  block_algorithm  fidelity_final  depth_after  cost_after  mean_hv  total_n_pareto  wall_clock_s
  nsga2                    0.1491        16.04       82.12   0.0520          121.84       52.41
  smsemoa                  0.1230        15.20       78.24   0.0596          171.72       50.95
  NSGA-II wins peak fidelity in every family (16/25 vs 9/25 seed-runs);
  SMS-EMOA gives a larger, more diverse front at no wall-clock cost —
  complementary, not a strict winner.

population; ref = that run's own normalized front's worst point + 0.1
margin). Fine for tracking one run's convergence (plot_moo_history's use

means something different per run. Same mechanism already flagged for
swap_add's outlier HV in "RESEARCH ANGLE CHOSEN AND IMPLEMENTED".

natural, unnormalized units) — purely additive, doesn't change existing
HV/Spread/Spacing behavior. Flows into metrics.json automatically. Added

algorithms' front points per block index (blocks are identical across
algorithms for the same circuit+seed), derives one shared min/max

Follow-up to the previous section's open item: investigated WHY
fidelity_driven_injection's greedy-pass win rate is non-monotonic in n
(92-96% @ n=8 -> 16-28% @ n=12 -> 72-88% @ n=16). Ended up finding two real,
previously-undocumented bugs in the injection stage instead of resolving
the original question -- both now fixed, and the fix's real-world impact is
large enough that the entire existing results_master.csv (1185 rows, every
run this project has ever recorded) is being treated as superseded.

STEP 1 -- fidelity-backend hypothesis REJECTED: the leading candidate
explanation (CIRCUIT_FIDELITY_SETTINGS' per-family backend/sample overrides
only activate above fidelity_exact_threshold=13, i.e. between n=12 and
n=16 for 3 of 5 families) was tested against weak_random as a control,
since weak_random's CIRCUIT_FIDELITY_SETTINGS entry is empty (no override
at any tested size). weak_random showed the IDENTICAL dip-then-recover
pattern (80-100% @ n=8 -> 0% @ n=12 -> 40-80% @ n=16, per-algorithm) as the
4 backend-switching families -- ruling out the fidelity backend as the
cause.

STEP 2 -- correction to an intermediate claim: n_kept_injections (in
results_master.csv) was initially treated as an independent lead (it
collapses to ~0 at n=12 for weak_random). Tracing the actual code
(optimise_circuit_pipeline, "kept_injections_used = kept1 if fid_i > fid_inj
else kept") showed this field is simply WHICHEVER path's kept-list won --
i.e. it's a restatement of injection_path_used flipping, not an independent
signal. Retracted as a lead; correctly identified as a symptom, not a
cause.

STEP 3 -- two real bugs found while digging into the actual mechanism:

(a) BLOCK-PAIR COVERAGE BUG (the more consequential one): stochastic_
injection, sa_injection (via _sa_generate_pool/_sa_rand_move), and
fidelity_driven_injection ALL hardcoded candidate gates to be drawn only
from blocks[0] and blocks[1] -- entirely ignoring every other block pair,
regardless of how many blocks a partition actually has. This was never
documented anywhere (checked CLAUDE.md/logs.txt/status.txt -- no mention).
Partitions above ~n=8 routinely have >2 blocks (e.g. weak_random's mean
block count runs 4.0 -> 7.6 -> 11.8 across n=8/12/16), so most inter-block
reconnection opportunities were structurally unreachable by ANY injection
strategy at scale -- a limitation of the whole injection stage, not
specific to sa vs stochastic vs greedy.

(b) BARRIER-MISCOUNTING BUG (weak_random-specific): extract_interblock_
gates classifies a gate as "inter-block" if its qubits span >1 block.
Qiskit's qc.barrier() with no explicit qubits touches ALL qubits by
default, so it ALWAYS trivially "spans multiple blocks" for any partition
with >1 block, regardless of whether any real entangling gate crosses a
block boundary. weak_random_circuit is the only one of the 5 generators
that calls .barrier() (after every depth layer) -- grep confirmed no other
generator does. Concretely verified on one real instance (weak_random,
n=8, seed=0): all 20 gates the pipeline reported as "inter-block gates
extracted for later reinjection" were barriers; the ACTUAL real 2-qubit
gate count crossing blocks was 0 (all 10 real cz gates were entirely
intra-block -- Louvain had already found a perfect zero-cut partition for
this instance). Barriers are unitary no-ops, so this bug never corrupted
fidelity_final directly, but it did inflate every "N inter-block gates
extracted" figure reported throughout this project's logs for weak_random,
and wasted a reinjection pass on inert instructions.

Both bugs found via direct code tracing plus minimal controlled repros
(a 4-qubit toy circuit confirming extract_interblock_gates's barrier
mislabeling; a Monte-Carlo check of stochastic_injection's accept-rate
baseline; a real weak_random n=8/12/16 probe pinning down the actual
mechanism) -- not by speculation. Neither bug explains the original
non-monotonic win-rate question: (a) is a constant limitation at every n
(not something that changes between n=12 and n=16 specifically), and (b)
only affects weak_random, yet all 4 barrier-free families show the same
reversal pattern. That original question remains OPEN -- see "Not done /
open" below.

QUANTIFIED IMPACT (before deciding whether to fix vs. just document):
re-ran the exact same weak_random/nsga2/point/stochastic/seed=0 probe used
to find the bugs, pre-fix vs. post-fix:
  n    old fidelity_final   new fidelity_final   real interblock gates
  8         0.0362               0.2265 (+526%)   2 (was reported as 20)
  12        0.3494               0.3495 (+0.03%)  0 (was reported as 20)
  16        0.3984               0.4004 (+0.5%)   0 (was reported as 20)
At n=8, the fix let fidelity_driven_injection reach gates between
previously-unreachable block pairs (e.g. cz(5,7), where qubit 5 lived in a
THIRD block never reachable under the old blocks[0]/blocks[1]-only code) --
a 6.3x fidelity swing for one seed. At n=12/16 the effect was negligible
for this seed specifically because the real (post-barrier-fix) interblock
gate count was genuinely zero -- nothing was there to recover regardless of
which block pairs were reachable. Confirms the fix's impact is highly
circuit/seed/partition-dependent, sometimes dramatic, and -- combined with
finding (b) -- that this project's own "N inter-block gates extracted"
statistics for weak_random were not measuring what they claimed to.
